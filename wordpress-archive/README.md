## About Us (/about/)
The Elder Mountain Volunteer Fire Department (EMVFD) is a fully volunteer force is a first line of defense
to the Elder Mountain Community. We are a unique community on the outskirts of Marion County and on the top
of a mountain. Our volunteer fire department is a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) non-profit organization staffed by
approximately 20 volunteers that are Firefighters, Emergency Medical Technicians (EMT), Medical First
Responders and support staff that is either trained or currently in training. The EMVFD responds to fire,
medical, rescue and other emergencies in its response area and maintains mutual aid agreements with
surrounding emergency units to supplement its resources. [photo: LifeForce training]
