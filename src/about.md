The Elder Mountain Fire Department (EMFD) is an all-volunteer department and the first line of defense for the Elder Mountain community.

Elder Mountain is a unique community on top of a mountain, and the department serves it in both Marion and Hamilton counties.

The department is a tax-exempt 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. About 20 volunteers serve as firefighters, emergency medical technicians (EMTs), medical first responders, and support staff, all either trained or in training.

## What we do

EMFD responds to fire, medical, rescue, and other emergencies in its response area. We maintain mutual aid agreements with the surrounding emergency services to add to our resources, and we help our neighbors when they call on us.
